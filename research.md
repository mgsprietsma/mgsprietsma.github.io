## Streamers around young stars

Young stars do not always grow in isolation. Some keep drawing material from their surroundings through long, narrow streamers of gas falling onto the disk. I map these streamers with molecular line observations and model the paths of the infalling gas to understand where it lands and how it changes the disk.
